Ashen Hunters is a third-person action RPG for Windows, built in Godot and still in development. Every new character starts on the Create Character screen, where you pick one of seven hunter classes. Each class has its own role, resource and difficulty.

## Warrior

The Warrior is the easiest class to start with: a melee fighter with sword and shield who spends stamina. The in-game advice sums it up: *stand where the enemy must come; take the swing on iron and answer inside its recovery.* Its weakness is archers, and anything behind you.

- **Starter skills:** Gate Sweep, Rim Strike, and Brace at level 3
- **Level 1 stats:** 140 health, 14 strength, 7 intellect

## Mage

The Mage is a ranged caster of frost and arcana that spends mana. The advice here: *find a safe casting window, then spend mana decisively.* It struggles with interruptions and with being surrounded.

- **Starter skills:** Arcane Missiles, Arcane Blast, Frost Nova at level 2, and Mending Light at level 3
- **Level 1 stats:** 80 health, 95 mana, 6 strength, 15 intellect

## What's next

PvP arenas from 1v1 up to 5v5 and a co-op overworld around the market town of Hollowmere are in development. Future posts will cover the other five classes and the combat itself.
