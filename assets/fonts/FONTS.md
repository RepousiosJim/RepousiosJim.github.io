# Fonts

Both fonts are self-hosted (Latin subset, variable weight) and licensed under the
[SIL Open Font License 1.1](https://openfontlicense.org).

- `big-shoulders-display-latin.woff2`: Big Shoulders Display (weights 700–900), by Patric King. Headings.
- `figtree-latin.woff2`: Figtree (weights 400–700), by Erik Kennedy. Body text.
